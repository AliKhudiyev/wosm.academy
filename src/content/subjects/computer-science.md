Computer science at WoSM Academy starts from the foundations. In Semester 1, Introduction to Informatics covers what computation is and how to write programs, and Digital Design and Computer Architecture I shows how digital circuits are designed and assembled into a working computer.

The later semesters of the [Foundation programme](/programme/) build on them: data structures and algorithms in Semesters 2 and 3, theory of computation and operating systems in Semester 4, and networks, databases and software engineering in Semester 5.

Each course page shows the full module description, including its prerequisites.
