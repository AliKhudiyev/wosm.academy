Computer science at WoSM Academy starts from the foundations. Our first courses cover what computation is and how information is represented, how digital circuits are designed and assembled into a working computer, and the discrete mathematics that the rest of the field is built on.

All three launch courses are introductory. Each course page lists its prerequisites, if it has any.
